01 About

I am an undergraduate in Electrical Engineering (Control) at Ferdowsi University of Mashhad. My work centers on dynamic modeling, feedback control, and how learning-based methods compare against classical controllers on real dynamical systems.

My long-term interest is guidance, navigation and control for spacecraft and space systems. I am building the foundations — modeling, estimation, optimal control, and modern C++ — that this field demands.
